Frequently Asked Questions

What 3D file formats are supported?
Stratum supports standard STL (binary and ASCII) model files. When you drop your file onto the configurator, our in-browser 3D engine renders an interactive preview with bounding dimensions, polygon triangle counts, and accurate volume estimates in real-time.
What is the maximum build volume available?
Our flagship Bambu Lab P1S printers feature an active build volume of 256 × 256 × 256 mm. If your model exceeds this build envelope, the configurator will alert you immediately so you can scale it proportionally or partition it into interlocking sections.
Which materials and colors are available on the floor?
We stock PLA (prototyping & aesthetics), PETG (durability & water resistance), ABS (high heat & rigidity), flexible TPU, and ultra-high-detail SLA Photopolymer Resin. We also support multi-color 3D printing via our 4-slot Bambu AMS system.
How is 3D print pricing calculated?
Unlike generic weight-only estimators, Stratum calculates quotes using true slicing metrics: exact part filament mass, tree support material, purge cycle allowance, machine run time, and post-processing. Bulk volume discounts are applied automatically for quantities > 1.
Where and when can I collect my finished parts?
Prints are fabricated and inspected at the School of Engineering and Technology (SET), Nagaland University workshop in Kohima. Standard turnaround is 1–2 business days. You can track your print's live status directly from your Stratum account dashboard.

Terms of Fabrication

1. Intellectual Property & File Rights

You retain full ownership of all 3D models and CAD data uploaded to Stratum. We only process and slice your files for the express purpose of estimating and manufacturing your order.

2. Printability & Geometric Tolerances

Additive manufacturing inherently features layer line textures and thermal contraction variances (±0.2mm standard FDM tolerance). Thin features under 0.8mm or steep un-supported overhangs may require customer iteration.

3. Workshop Inspection & Pickup

Orders are completed at the SET Nagaland University workshop. Parts are inspected against specifications prior to notification. Users are requested to verify dimensions upon handover.

4. Academic & Research Prototype Policy

Stratum prioritizes student engineering projects, faculty prototyping, and verified fabrication requests with subsidized material recovery rates.

Privacy & File Retention Policy

1. Data Collection

We collect basic contact information (name, phone number, academic department) and your uploaded STL files solely to coordinate fabrication and provide live order tracking updates.

2. Model Storage & Confidentiality

Model files are stored securely in Google Cloud Storage / Firebase with authenticated access controls. Your design files are never shared with external parties or commercial third parties.

3. Account Security

Authentication credentials are encrypted using Firebase Auth standards. Users can delete or request deletion of their order history and stored mesh files upon order completion.
